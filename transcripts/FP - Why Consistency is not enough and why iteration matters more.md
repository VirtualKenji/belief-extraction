# FP - Why Consistency is not enough and why iteration matters more

okay, why consistency is not enough and why iteration matters more. This is actually something I learned over time when I first started writing online. Remember the 90-day challenge - don't take a day off for 90 days to do the same tasks, and then 100% you'll get to where you are. Right? It's not a hundred percent, but basically they're talking about the 90-day challenge, and if you get these three months done by doing the same tasks every day, then the way they sold it was like, "Oh, you'll be unstoppable if you're just consistent." And I did it, it helped, and I actually tracked maybe a thousand something days. I didn't do it straight, but I was extremely consistent.

What I actually figured out is that I progressed faster when I stopped tracking basically right. And I realized that when I talked to Dariah, his feedback for me was basically like, "I was good at processes, but I wasn't good at finding out cause and effect things." Right? And I didn't understand what he meant. But what I was basically doing was I was very consistent over time with very little iteration, and I didn't realize why I was stuck. I didn't realize why, "Oh, I'm writing every day, improving my writing, I'm doing all these little things. I do two hours of whatever every day, I have my habits every day, I visualize every day, I do all that. But I do my grad 2 is like, 'Why am I stuck? Why is my income the same way?' Right? I didn't realize that like consistency is sort of just getting your foot in the door. Like everybody at a high level is like, 'Ex, that's not the lever that needs to be pulled. It's like the first lever that you're supposed to hold.' But everybody ahead of me is like they're not only consistent, they're like, 'Consistent plus plus plus plus.'
